This is an exploration project born from a simple idea: creativity doesn't depend on extraordinary subjects, but on the way we choose to see the ordinary. Through unexpected transformations and playful interactions, everyday desk objects reveal new possibilities. A celebration of curiosity, imagination, and the creative potential hidden in the ordinary. Created in collaboration with Ilgin İçözü (sound design).
